A note about speech recognition

This free practice tool checks whether your browser recognizes the target word. It does not score sounds, accents, or phonetic pronunciation. Recognition can make mistakes.

Use Chrome on desktop or Android for the best chance of support. Safari support varies. Other browsers may not support the Web Speech API. Microphone access requires HTTPS or localhost. Your browser may send audio to its speech service, which usually requires an internet connection. This app does not store recordings.

Some speech services merge rapid repetitions or omit repeated words. We count each word the service returns, but cannot recover words it omits. Short pauses help. Sessions restart automatically while practice is active; repeated service errors stop practice so you can retry.
